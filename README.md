# SALES ANALYSIS DASHBOARD

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

The Sales Analysis Dashboard provides interactive visualizations and KPIs to help identity business trends , top-performing products and customer purchasing patterns . It is developed using the Superstore Sales Dataset to analyse sales performance across products , region , ship modes , customer , category , segment and time.

## 2. BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

The Dashboard was developed to :
- Highlight top performing customers , products and category.
- Analyse the sales across product categories.
- Track sales trends throughout month and year.
- Support data driven strategic and operational decision.

## 3. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs)

  The following KPIs are tracked within dashboard :
- TOTAL SALES - Aggregate sales generated during the specific period of time ( given in dataset).
- TOTAL ORDERS - total numbers of order shipped .
- AVERAGE SALES - Sales per order.
- AVERAGE SHIP DAYS - Duration between order placed and shipment.

 ## 5. KEY INSIGHT

 - West region contribute largest share of sales.
